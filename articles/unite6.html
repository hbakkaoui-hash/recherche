ABSTRACT

Context. Units 1–3 treat the scale axes (\(\psi\) spatial, \(\chi\) temporal) as internal targets of a sigma model — explicitly not spacetime dimensions — so as to avoid ghosts and closed timelike curves (result F2). Problem. We study the opposite choice: promoting the scale axis to a genuine fifth spacetime dimension. Two questions: does this embedding regenerate the corpus action? and does it allow predicting the coupling \(\beta\) rather than fitting it? Method. Kaluza–Klein reduction of a warped 5D metric along a compact scale circle, with symbolic verification of all the tensor algebra, spectral computation of the KK tower, and heat-kernel expansion of the deformed operator. Results. (i) Generative [proof] [numerical] : the KK reduction reproduces the corpus structure — induced non-minimal coupling \(f(\sigma)=a(\sigma)^2\), trigonometric form \(f=1+\beta\cos\sigma\) forced by compactness, \(V\)–\(f\) locking giving exactly \(V=\alpha(1-\cos\sigma)\), and the F2 health criterion re-derived (spacelike scale axis \(\to\) healthy; timelike \(\to\) ghost); for \(a=e^{\sigma/\ell}\) the geometry is exactly AdS\(_5\), the scale playing the role of the holographic / renormalization-group direction, \(\partial_\sigma\) its dilatation isometry and \(\sigma\to-\sigma\) a scale mirror (cousin of T-duality). The discreteness of the KK tower further provides a bridge (candidate, not derived) toward the \(\tau_0,\varepsilon\) clock of Unit 1. (ii) Cost [proof] : the promotion reopens the ghost / closed-timelike-curve question that F2 closed by postulate (closed here by the choice of spatial signature), and closing the warp into a loop requires a violation of the null energy condition (NEC) in the bulk, localized at the Planck pole \(P\). (iii) Conjecture [conjecture] : this exotic source is identified with vacuum Casimir energy — correct tensor structure \(\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(1,1,1,1,-4)\), correct sign (violating the NEC) for periodic fields, correct Planckian magnitude (self-consistent once \(\ell\sim\ell_5\)). (iv) Rigorous negative [proof] : vacuum energy does not predict \(\beta\). The third heat-kernel coefficient of the warped operator is non-zero, \(b_3(\beta)\simeq0.146\,\beta^2\), so the 5D Casimir energy is ultraviolet-divergent at the very order that would fix \(\beta\); the value \(\beta_\star\) depends on the renormalization scheme. The corpus \(\sigma\)-model formulation does not have this bulk divergence — a point in its favor. Conclusion. The 5D embedding is a structural origin (generative), not a more predictive replacement; \(\beta\) remains a coupling constrained by the CMB, as in the corpus.

Keywords: scale duality; Kaluza–Klein reduction; warped extra dimension; AdS\(_5\) / renormalization direction; induced non-minimal coupling; Casimir energy; null energy condition; Seeley–DeWitt coefficients; natural inflation.

Preliminary note. This unit explores a geometric choice deliberately opposite to that of the corpus, for the sole purpose of testing whether the scale-duality action has a dimensional origin. The verdict is mixed and we record it as such: the embedding is remarkably generative, but it does not improve the predictive power of the framework and, on one precise point (the ultraviolet behavior of the vacuum), it is less well-behaved than the original \(\sigma\)-model formulation. The formalization was carried out in dialogue with analytical-assistance systems, under the direction of the author, who bears full responsibility for the scientific content.

Introduction: how and why a fifth scale dimension

The corpus choice, and its opposite

The corpus (Units 1–3) rests on one hypothesis: the logarithmic scale axis \(u=\ln(\lambda/\ell_P)\), usually non-compact, is in fact a circle, via the projection \(\psi=2\arctan u\in(-\pi,\pi]\), which identifies the Planck pole (\(\psi=0\), denoted \(P\)) and a “unified” pole (\(\psi=\pm\pi\) identified, denoted \(\infty^{*}\)). One point is crucial there: these scale circles are internal targets of a Euclidean sigma model, not spacetime dimensions. This is result F2: since \(\chi\) is not a timelike coordinate, there is neither ghost nor closed timelike curve. This choice is conservative: it imports the duality structure without touching the spacetime signature.

The question of this unit is the opposite choice. What if one took the scale axis literally, as a genuine fifth spacetime dimension? This choice is bolder: it reopens the ghost and CTC question that F2 closed by postulate. But it offers a possible payoff: if the corpus action descends from a 5D geometry by dimensional reduction, then its particular form (the locked non-minimal coupling, the bounded potential) ceases to be a choice and becomes a consequence. This is the generative stake we test.

Why “spacetime”: the relativistic locking

The corpus has two scale axes: a spatial axis \(\psi\) (length scale \(\lambda\)) and a temporal axis \(\chi\) (duration scale \(\tau\)). A fifth dimension is unique. For a single dimension to carry both the spatial scale and the temporal scale, these two scales must be locked: this is the relativistic locking \(\tau=\lambda/c\), i.e. a dynamical exponent \(z=1\). Under this locking, the two axes \(\psi\) and \(\chi\) collapse onto a single diagonal coordinate, which we denote \(\sigma\). The fifth dimension is thus spacetime in the precise sense that it simultaneously encodes both scales, tied by \(c\) — not in the sense of a mixed signature.

dictionary with Units 2–3 In this unit, \(\beta\) denotes the amplitude of the 5D non-minimal coupling along the diagonal axis \(\sigma\). Under the identification \(\sigma\leftrightarrow(\psi=\chi)\), it coincides with the diagonal non-minimal coupling of the corpus, i.e. twice the per-axis coupling. The diagonal closure threshold \(\beta_c^{\mathrm{diag}}=-1/3\) thus corresponds to the per-axis threshold \(-1/6\) of Units 2–3, and the viable band translates accordingly. All the formulas below are written with the diagonal \(\sigma\) and stay within the “single \(\beta\)” convention.

One-point compactification and duality involution

The compactified scale axis identifies the infinitely small and the infinitely large. The projection \(\sigma=2\arctan u\) sends \(u=0\) (Planck) to \(\sigma=0\) (\(P\), true vacuum) and \(u\to\pm\infty\) (extreme scales) to \(\sigma=\pm\pi\) (\(\infty^{*}\), false vacuum), the two poles \(\pm\pi\) being identified. The scale duality \(\lambda\mapsto\ell_P^2/\lambda\) is the involution \(\mathrm{D}:\sigma\mapsto-\sigma\), whose \(P\) and \(\infty^{*}\) are the two fixed points. It is this \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) involution that will constrain the form of the warp (§4).

The geometric anchor: scale as a holographic direction

The choice is not gratuitous. A 5D metric warped along the scale coordinate, \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:metric} ds_5^2 \;=\; a(\sigma)^2\,\eta_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu \;+\; \ell^2\,d\sigma^2, \qquad \mu,\nu=0,1,2,3, \end{equation}\] takes, for the exponential choice \(a(\sigma)=e^{\sigma/\ell}\), the exact form of anti-de Sitter space AdS\(_5\) in Poincaré coordinates. Now, in the AdS/CFT correspondence, the radial coordinate of AdS\(_5\) is the energy scale of the boundary theory, i.e. the renormalization-group direction. Identifying the scale axis with a fifth dimension is therefore to rediscover the holographic intuition: “moving in scale” \(=\) “moving in a dimension”. The compactification into a circle (instead of the radial half-line of AdS) is what distinguishes our framework and breaks the exact dilatation, generating a hierarchy of scales between \(P\) and \(\infty^{*}\). The program is then clear: reduce [eq:metric] over \(\sigma\) and check what the 4D inherits.

Formal framework: warped metric and Kaluza–Klein reduction

5D arena The fundamental spacetime is \(\mathcal{M}_5=\mathcal{M}_{3+1}\times S^1_\sigma\), where \(S^1_\sigma\) is the scale circle (period \(2\pi\)), \(\sigma=0\equiv P\), \(\sigma=\pm\pi\equiv\infty^{*}\). The metric is [eq:metric] with \(a(\sigma)>0\) periodic. The coordinate \(\sigma\) is spacelike (\(g_{\sigma\sigma}=+\ell^2\)), the only choice compatible with the absence of ghosts (§4, F2).

[proof] (verified by symbolic computation) For the metric [eq:metric], the non-zero components of the 5D Einstein tensor are, writing \('=d/d\sigma\) and \(\ell=1\), \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:einstein} G^t_{\ t}=G^x_{\ x}=3\!\left(\frac{a''}{a}+\frac{a'^2}{a^2}\right), \qquad G^\sigma_{\ \sigma}=6\,\frac{a'^2}{a^2}, \qquad R_5=-8\frac{a''}{a}-12\frac{a'^2}{a^2}. \end{equation}\] The reduction of the 5D Einstein–Hilbert action \(\int d^5x\sqrt{-g_5}\,R_5\) over the circle produces, in the 4D Jordan frame, a tensor–scalar system whose non-minimal coupling is the square of the warp, \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:fa2} \boxed{\,f(\sigma)=a(\sigma)^2\,}\qquad\text{(gravity induced by the warping).} \end{equation}\]

General geometric properties

Before the reduction, the metric [eq:metric] has a geometric structure that clarifies why the identification scale \(=\) dimension is natural — and what it costs.

AdS\(_5\) and the dilatation isometry

[proof] In the current region, the exponential warp \(a(\sigma)=e^{\sigma/\ell}\) gives \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:ads} ds_5^2=e^{2\sigma/\ell}\,\eta_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu+\ell^2 d\sigma^2,\qquad R_5=-\frac{20}{\ell^2}\ \ \text{(constant)}, \end{equation}\] i.e. exactly anti-de Sitter space AdS\(_5\) of radius \(\ell\). The shift \(\sigma\to\sigma+\lambda_0\ell,\ x^\mu\to e^{-\lambda_0}x^\mu\) leaves \(ds_5^2\) invariant: this is the dilatation, whose Killing vector is \(\partial_\sigma\). [to check] (standard reading) the scale coordinate \(\sigma\) is then the radial / holographic direction of AdS, identified with the renormalization scale; the Noether charge of \(\partial_\sigma\) is a genuine scale momentum — the dynamical analogue of the topological winding charge of Units 2–3. The intuition “scale \(=\) dimension” therefore lands on marked-out ground, not out of nowhere.

The duality involution: a scale mirror

[proof] The map \(\mathcal{D}:\sigma\to-\sigma\), i.e. \(\lambda\to\ell_P^2/\lambda\), exchanges ultraviolet and infrared — the analogue of string T-duality. Its fixed points are exactly \(\sigma=0\) (\(P\)) and \(\sigma=\pi\) (\(\infty^{*}\)): a scale mirror that leaves the Planck point and the confluence point invariant. Keeping the temporal axis independent, one recovers the Klein group \(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2\) of Units 2–3.

Compactness breaks dilatation — and that is an asset

[proof] A periodic warp cannot be the exact exponential [eq:ads] (which is not periodic): periodicity forces the bounded form \(a^2=1+\beta\cos\sigma\) and therefore breaks the exact dilatation isometry. Far from being a defect, this breaking is precisely what generates a hierarchy of scales (\(\ell_P\) versus the cosmological scale) between \(P\) and \(\infty^{*}\), instead of an exact scale invariance that nothing observes.

Kaluza–Klein tower and a candidate origin for the clock (bridge to Unit 1)

[conjecture] Since the extra dimension is a circle, every field there has a discrete spectrum (§6, Fig. 3): zero mode \(=\) 4D physics, massive modes quantized by \(1/\ell\). This discreteness is the geometric candidate for the origin of the intermittent clock of Unit 1: the oscillation in the compact direction \(\sigma\) would play the role of the pendulum whose average gives the existence factor \(\varepsilon=E(k)/K(k)\) and the period \(\tau_0=2\pi\sqrt{\omega/\alpha}\). The scale coherence is encouraging — the fundamental KK frequency \(\sim 1/\ell\) fixes a period \(\sim\ell\), and \(\ell\sim\sqrt{\omega/\alpha}\) recovers the right order of magnitude for \(\tau_0\) — but this is not a derivation: we have not established it in this work. It is the direction that would turn a conjecture of Unit 1 into a result, and we record it as such, without overstating it. [open]

Degeneracy at the confluence point

[open] The exponential reading [eq:ads] would suggest a degeneracy at \(\infty^{*}\), where the two limits of the warp (\(a\to\infty\) and \(a\to0\)) would be glued together. But the periodic warp regularizes this gluing: at \(\sigma=\pi\), \(a^2=1-\beta\) stays finite (the 4D slice does not degenerate). The apparent singularity of the exponential is therefore an artifact of its global extrapolation; the full consistency of the stereographic compactification of scale with the warp dynamics, however, remains to be established.

The generative content

Trigonometric form forced by compactness

[proof] Since the coordinate \(\sigma\) is a compact angle, \(f(\sigma)\) must be \(2\pi\)-periodic; invariance under the duality involution \(\mathrm{D}:\sigma\to-\sigma\) imposes parity. The lowest non-trivial harmonic form is therefore \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:ftrig} f(\sigma)=1+\beta\cos\sigma,\qquad a(\sigma)=\sqrt{1+\beta\cos\sigma}, \end{equation}\] with \(f(P)=1+\beta\) (true vacuum) and \(f(\infty^{*})=1-\beta\) (false vacuum). Positivity \(f>0\) requires \(|\beta|<1\). This is exactly the non-minimal structure of the corpus, here forced by the compact geometry rather than postulated.

\(V\)–\(f\) locking: the bounded potential descends from the warp

[proof] We seek the simplest 4D potential compatible with compactness: periodic, affine in \(f\) (hence generated by the same source as the coupling), and vanishing at the true vacuum, \(V(P)=0\). These three conditions determine it uniquely. Writing \(V=A+Bf\) and imposing \(V(P)=A+B(1+\beta)=0\), one obtains \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:Vlock} \boxed{\,V(\sigma)=\frac{\alpha}{\beta}\,\bigl(1+\beta-f\bigr)=\alpha\,(1-\cos\sigma)\,}, \qquad V(P)=0,\quad V(\infty^{*})=2\alpha, \end{equation}\] i.e. exactly the bounded potential of the corpus. A brane-tension term, which would behave like \(a^4=f^2=(1+\beta\cos\sigma)^2\), would inject a second harmonic \(\cos2\sigma\) (via \(\cos^2\sigma\)) absent from [eq:Vlock]: the potential therefore does not descend from a brane tension, but from a source in \(a^2\). The coupling and the potential thus share a single geometric origin.

The cost, and the F2 criterion re-derived from 5D

[proof] The cost of the promotion. Making scale a genuine dimension reopens the question that the corpus had closed by postulate (F2): a timelike, compact extra dimension generates closed timelike curves and ghosts. We close it again, but only at the price of a signature choice. The kinetic term of \(\sigma\) arising from the reduction carries the sign of \(g_{\sigma\sigma}\). For spacelike \(\sigma\) (\(g_{\sigma\sigma}=+\ell^2\)), the 4D kinetic term has a healthy sign; for timelike \(\sigma\), it becomes a ghost (opposite sign, verified by the determinant of the induced metric). The corpus result F2 — absence of ghost and CTC — therefore appears here as a consequence of the signature choice of the fifth dimension, where the corpus posited it. This choice (spacelike) is that of Postulate [post:arene].

The cost: null energy condition violation at the Planck pole

[proof] For a periodic warp [eq:ftrig] (which must come back down after rising, in order to close) to solve the 5D Einstein equations, the bulk source must violate the null energy condition along \(\sigma\). The relevant combination is read off [eq:einstein]: \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:NEC} \kappa^2\,(\rho+p_\sigma)=G^\sigma_{\ \sigma}-G^t_{\ t}=-3\,(\ln a)'' =\frac{3\beta\,(\beta+\cos\sigma)}{2\,(1+\beta\cos\sigma)^2}. \end{equation}\] For the physical sign \(\beta<0\), this quantity is negative (NEC violated) in the neighborhood of \(P\) (\(\sigma=0\): \(3\beta/[2(1+\beta)]<0\), minimum of the warp) and positive (healthy) in the neighborhood of \(\infty^{*}\) (Fig. 1). The violation is therefore localized exactly at the Planck pole.

The exotic source: vacuum Casimir energy

The decisive question is the nature of this source. Is it an ad hoc defect (fatal), or a natural quantum effect? We show that it is Casimir energy.

Tensor structure

[proof] Decomposing the required source \(\kappa^2 T^A_{\ B}=G^A_{\ B}\) into a trace part (bulk cosmological constant) and a traceless part, the latter equals \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:traceless} \tau^A_{\ B}=\frac{3}{5}\,(\ln a)''\;\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}(1,1,1,1,-4), \end{equation}\] i.e. exactly the structure of a conformal (Casimir) tensor on a circle. This form is imposed by the isotropy of the four extended dimensions plus one compact dimension: it is therefore partly kinematic, and the physical content resides in the sign and the magnitude.

Sign

[to check] (standard result) A massless periodic field in 5D has a negative Casimir energy , \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:rhocas} \rho_{\mathrm{Cas}}=-\frac{\Gamma(5/2)\,\zeta(5)}{\pi^{5/2}\,L^5}<0, \end{equation}\] which gives \(\rho+p_\sigma<0\) along the circle: precisely the NEC violation required by [eq:NEC]. The “periodic” nature is that of the Kaluza–Klein fields  themselves and of the 5D graviton: no new ingredient (Fig. 2).

Magnitude and self-consistency

[numerical] The required exotic density at \(P\) is \(\simeq 0.64/(\kappa^2\ell^2)\). Equating it to the Casimir density of a circle of radius \(\ell\), \(\sim 1/\ell^5\), imposes \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:selfcons} \ell\sim 1/M_5=\ell_5, \end{equation}\] i.e. a circle of Planckian size — which is tautologically the case, since the pole \(P\) is the Planck point. No hierarchy, no fine-tuning: the scale circle is held closed by its own zero-point energy, exactly at Planck. This cleanly closes the quantum \(\leftrightarrow\) gravity thread of the corpus: the same pole \(P\) that cancels the gravitational potential (\(V=0\)) is held closed by the quantum vacuum energy. [conjecture]

Does vacuum energy predict \(\beta\)? The heat-kernel obstruction

If the warp is held by the Casimir energy, can one go further and predict \(\beta\) as the value that minimizes the vacuum energy, rather than fitting it to the CMB? This is the heaviest computation of this work — explicit KK spectrum, Casimir energy regularized by the spectral zeta function, and a test of dynamical stabilization of the warp — and we validate each step before drawing a conclusion. We show that the answer is no, for a rigorous reason.

KK spectrum on the warped circle

[numerical] A 5D scalar field separated into modes, \(\Phi=\sum_n\phi_n(x)\psi_n(\sigma)\), leads to the Sturm–Liouville problem \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:SL} -\bigl(a^4\psi_n'\bigr)'=\lambda_n\,a^2\,\psi_n,\qquad \lambda_n=M_n^2\ell^2, \end{equation}\] on the circle, with periodic conditions. The zero mode (\(\lambda_0=0\)) is the 4D graviton; the KK tower compresses as \(|\beta|\) grows, the high modes tending to \((n/L_*)^2\) with the effective size \(L_*=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint d\sigma/a>1\) (Fig. 3). The fundamental mode stays pinned at \(M_1=1/\ell\) for all \(\beta\).

Validated machinery, and the nature of the stabilization

[proof] The total Casimir energy per 4D volume, for a periodic scalar field, is \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:Ecas} \mathcal{E}(\beta)=\frac{1}{120\pi^2\ell^5}\,\zeta_L(-5/2;\beta),\qquad \zeta_L(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}\lambda_n^{-s}, \end{equation}\] where \(\zeta_L\) is the spectral zeta function of the operator [eq:SL]. Validation at \(\beta=0\): since the spectrum there is exactly \(\lambda_n=n^2\) (doubly degenerate, cf. Table 1), one recovers \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:val0} \zeta_L(-5/2;0)=2\zeta(-5)=-\tfrac{1}{126}, \end{equation}\] — the zeta-regularization machinery is therefore correct in this controlled limit, and the energy there is negative (attractive Casimir, consistent with \(\rho_{\mathrm{Cas}}<0\) of §5).

The nature of the problem. The lift \(\sigma\to\sigma+\pi\) exchanges \(\beta\to-\beta\) while being an isometry of the circle; the energy is therefore even, \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:even} \mathcal{E}(\beta)=\mathcal{E}(-\beta)=\mathcal{E}_0+\mathcal{E}_2\,\beta^2+\mathcal{E}_4\,\beta^4+\cdots \qquad(\text{verified: }L_*(-0.3)=L_*(+0.3)). \end{equation}\] Decisive consequence: the Casimir energy cannot fix \(\beta\) at first order. The uniform circle \(\beta=0\) is an extremum, and \(\beta\) appears only at second order — this is a moduli stabilization problem (à la Coleman–Weinberg), where the Casimir contribution \(\mathcal{E}_2\) competes with the (positive) gravitational cost of curving the circle, \(\beta_\star\) emerging from the balance at orders \(\beta^2,\beta^4\). It is precisely this framework that the next subsection defeats: the coefficient \(\mathcal{E}_2\) itself is ill-defined (divergent).

The obstruction: ultraviolet divergence at the order that matters

[proof] [numerical] In Schrödinger form, [eq:SL] becomes \(-v''+Q(\sigma)v=\lambda v\) with the effective potential (closed form) \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:Q} Q(\sigma)=\frac{3}{16}\,\frac{f'^2}{f}+\frac{3}{4}\,f''. \end{equation}\] The Seeley–DeWitt coefficients  of this operator control the poles of \(\zeta_L(s)\): \(\zeta_L(s)\) has a pole at \(s=\tfrac{1}{2}-k\) with residue \(\propto b_k\), where \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:bk} b_1=-\!\int Q\,dt,\qquad b_2=\tfrac12\!\int Q^2\,dt,\qquad b_3=\int\!\Bigl(-\tfrac16 Q^3+\tfrac1{12}\dot Q^2\Bigr)dt . \end{equation}\] The energy lives at \(s=-5/2\), i.e. \(k=3\). The computation gives (Fig. 4) \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:b3} \boxed{\,b_3(\beta)\simeq 0.146\,\beta^2\neq 0\quad(\beta\neq0),\qquad b_3(0)=0\,} \end{equation}\] the dominant term coming from \(\int\dot Q^2\,dt\propto\beta^2\) (a Korteweg–de Vries invariant, robustly non-zero). Chain of consequences, each link verified: \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:chain} b_3\neq0\;\Longrightarrow\;\zeta_L\ \text{has a pole at}\ s=-\tfrac52 \;\Longrightarrow\;\mathcal{E}\propto\zeta_L(-\tfrac52)\ \text{diverges} \;\Longrightarrow\;\text{a bulk counterterm is required}. \end{equation}\]

Consequence and escape route

[proof] The vacuum energy that would fix \(\beta\) therefore requires a local counterterm depending on \(\beta\) (a higher-curvature term in the 5D action). Since its coefficient is free, the renormalized energy carries a scale dependence \(\propto b_3(\beta)\ln\mu\): the value \(\beta_\star\) depends on the renormalization scheme. This behavior is the known one of Casimir energies in warped 5D (Randall–Sundrum type) , which renormalize brane tensions and the bulk cosmological constant. The only escape route would be a supersymmetric field content, where \(\sum_F(-1)^F b_3=0\) would render the energy finite; but \(\beta_\star\) would then remain content-dependent. In all cases: no parameter-free prediction. [conjecture]

A point in favor of the \(\sigma\)-model

[proof] The original corpus formulation treats \(\sigma\) as an internal target: there is then no bulk circle on which fields wind, hence no analogous Casimir divergence. On this precise point, the \(\sigma\)-model is better behaved than its 5D promotion. The dimensional embedding illuminates the origin of the action, but does not provide better control of \(\beta\).

Discussion

What the 5D embedding brings, and what it does not

The assessment is mixed and instructive. The embedding regenerates the corpus structure: the non-minimal coupling \(f=a^2\), its trigonometric form, the \(V\)–\(f\) locking, and the F2 health criterion become consequences of the warped 5D geometry rather than choices. It also provides a natural origin for the required exotic source: vacuum Casimir energy, with correct sign, structure and Planckian magnitude. But it does not improve predictive power: the coupling \(\beta\) cannot be predicted from the vacuum energy, whose relevant part is UV-divergent. The embedding is thus to be read as a structural origin story, not as a more fundamental framework replacing the \(\sigma\)-model.

Positioning in the literature and related work

None of the physical bricks mobilized here is proper to this work: each has an identifiable lineage, which we first survey, before isolating what is, properly, a matter of assembly.

  • Scale as a variable. Promoting resolution/scale to the rank of a coordinate is the guiding idea of Nottale’s scale relativity ; on the holographic side, the identification “radial coordinate \(=\) energy scale / renormalization direction” [eq:metric] is an established fact of AdS/CFT .

  • Inflation. Our inflationary sector (periodic non-minimal coupling \(f=1+\beta\cos\), \(V\propto 1-\cos\)) coincides with the periodic non-minimal-coupling natural inflation of Ferreira–Notari–Simeon  and Simeon , which brings “bare” natural inflation  — today disfavored by the data — back within the \((n_s,r)\) contours at the GUT scale.

  • c-function / directed flow. The link “null energy condition \(\Rightarrow\) a monotone function along the flow” goes back to the Zamolodchikov c-theorem  and its holographic version ; our cosmological c-function is an FRW adaptation of it.

  • Bulk Casimir. The Casimir energy of a compact dimension, regularized over the KK tower, and its tendency to stabilize or destabilize the radius, are classical since Appelquist–Chodos .

  • Scale duality. The UV \(\leftrightarrow\) IR inversion with a minimal length (“zero-point length”, \(ds\to\ell_P^2/ds\)) of Padmanabhan , a cousin of string T-duality, prefigures our involution \(\mathcal{D}:\sigma\to-\sigma\) [eq:metric].

  • Relational clock. The bridge whereby gravity modulates the rate of an internal clock is the Page–Wootters formalism  (sector developed in Units 1 and 4–5).

  • Closure. That a regular black hole requires an energy-condition violation (de Sitter core) is established since Bardeen  and Hayward .

None of the ingredients above is new in isolation, and we claim no new observable: the predicted \((n_s,r)\) are degenerate with non-minimal natural inflation . What is proper to the corpus comes down to three points.

  1. The unifying move. Compactifying both scale axes — spatial and temporal — as internal targets of a single scalar–tensor \(\sigma\)-model (\(f=1+\beta(\cos\psi+\cos\chi)\), \(V=\alpha(2-\cos\psi-\cos\chi)\)), then realizing the compact object as a circle/torus (Units 1–4) , a sphere (Unit 5) , or a warped fifth dimension (this Unit). We have not found this precise construction elsewhere: it is in the spirit of Nottale  and of holographic RG , but its realization as a scale \(\sigma\)-model is proper to the corpus.

  2. Over-determination. A single coupling \(\beta\), fixed once by the CMB, must simultaneously satisfy several sectors (inflation, static closure, false-vacuum metastability, clock freeze, core stability, RG flow), through crossed internal identities — the same threshold \(\beta_c\) pivoting two sectors at once. This is the methodological signature of the programme, and its possibly fortuitous character is explicitly left [conjecture] / [open].

  3. The model-internal exact forms. A closed-form Hawking–Moss action vanishing at the threshold, the spectral validation \(\zeta_L(-5/2;0)=-1/126\), the heat-kernel coefficient \(b_3(\beta)\simeq0.146\,\beta^2\), the existence factor \(\varepsilon=E(k)/K(k)\): exact results, but internal to the framework.

In sum, the contribution is not a distinctive prediction but a grammar: a single compact geometry that reorganizes disparate phenomena under a handful of numbers, each statement carrying its epistemic status. Anything distinctive lives at GUT/Planck curvature (screened) or remains structurally real but silent in the currently accessible spectrum.

Conclusion

Promoting the scale axis to the rank of a compactified spacetime fifth dimension — the inverse of the corpus choice — lands first on marked-out geometric ground: the warped metric is AdS\(_5\), the scale is its renormalization direction, \(\partial_\sigma\) is its dilatation isometry, and the involution \(\sigma\to-\sigma\) is a scale mirror (cousin of T-duality) leaving \(P\) and \(\infty^{*}\) fixed. Closing this scale line into a loop breaks the exact dilatation — which, far from being a defect, generates the hierarchy of scales.

The embedding is then generative: the Kaluza–Klein reduction reproduces the non-minimal coupling \(f=a^2\), its trigonometric form imposed by compactness, the \(V\)–\(f\) locking giving \(V=\alpha(1-\cos\sigma)\), and F2 health. It even offers a bridge toward Unit 1: the discreteness of the KK tower is the geometric candidate for the origin of the intermittent clock (\(\tau_0,\varepsilon\)) — coherent in order of magnitude, but not derived here, and we record it as such.

Two costs, in all honesty. On the one hand, the promotion reopens the ghost and closed-timelike-curve question that F2 closed by postulate; we close it again, but only by keeping the scale on the space side. On the other hand, closing the circle requires a null-energy-condition violation at the Planck pole — a cost that naturally identifies with vacuum Casimir energy, with correct sign, structure and Planckian magnitude. But the embedding does not improve predictive power: the vacuum energy does not fix \(\beta\), its relevant part being UV-divergent (\(b_3\neq0\)), so that \(\beta_\star\) depends on the scheme; on this point, the original \(\sigma\)-model is even better behaved.

The 5D embedding is therefore an illuminating structural origin — it recovers and grounds the form of the framework — and not a more fundamental replacement. This is the price at which, with the honest labeling of each statement, a geometric intuition can be evaluated for what it is worth: here, a fine origin and a promising bridge, but not a prediction.
